Reading｜常見詞彙與詞性變化

先掌握常在議論、社會、科技、環境文章出現的字。這是起步清單，不代表 DSE 官方必考字表。

20 個核心字附中文意思noun / verb / adjective

溫習方法：先遮住中文理解句子，再留意同一字根的不同詞性。

Word中文Noun 名詞Verb 動詞Adjective 形容詞

Reading 解題小方法

  1. 先看題目，圈出人物、時間、原因等關鍵字。
  2. 回文章找同義改寫，不要只找一模一樣的字。
  3. 看前後句判斷生字：轉折、因果、例子通常有提示。
  4. 選答案時確認整句意思，避免只憑單一關鍵字。

猜字義的線索

  • but / however：前後意思可能相反。
  • because / therefore：因果關係。
  • such as / for example：後面通常是例子。
  • 前綴 / 字尾：un- 常表示否定；-tion 常見於名詞；-able 常見於形容詞。

Writing｜格式、Sample 與拓展方法

DSE 寫作不只是用難字；更重要是切合題目、內容具體、段落清楚、語氣和格式正確。

常見格式

1. Article 文章

標題 → 引入主題 → 2–3 個主體段 → 總結 / 呼籲

語氣：吸引讀者，可用 rhetorical question。

常見格式

2. Email / Letter 電郵／信件

Dear … → 開場交代目的 → 分段回應所有要點 → 結尾 → Yours sincerely / Best wishes

語氣視乎對象：朋友較自然，老師／機構較正式。

常見格式

3. Report 報告

To / From / Date / Subject → Introduction → Findings → Recommendations

語氣客觀，提出觀察和可行建議。

常見格式

4. Proposal 建議書

Title → Purpose → Proposed measures → Expected benefits → Conclusion

重點：建議要具體，解釋如何實行及有何好處。

常見題型

5. Argumentative / Opinion

Introduction（立場）→ Reason 1 + explanation → Reason 2 + example → Counterargument（可選）→ Conclusion

不要只列點；每個理由都要解釋。

常見題型

6. Speech 演講稿

Good morning everyone → 引入 → 分點論述 → 總結 → Thank you

可直接稱呼聽眾，語氣自然、有感染力。

如何把內容拓展？用 PEEL

P · Point

提出清楚論點。

Schools should promote outdoor activities.

E · Explain

解釋為甚麼。

This is because regular exercise can improve students' physical health and reduce stress.

E · Example

加入具體例子。

For example, schools could organise weekly sports sessions after class.

L · Link

扣回題目／段落主旨。

Therefore, outdoor activities should be an essential part of school life.

Sample｜Should schools encourage students to do more exercise?

以下是短篇示範，用來觀察段落結構；正式考試要按題目要求及字數發展。

Why Exercise Matters

Nowadays, many students spend long hours sitting in classrooms or using electronic devices. Some people believe that schools should encourage students to exercise more. I strongly agree with this view.

First, regular exercise helps students stay healthy. It strengthens the body and reduces the risk of health problems. For example, schools can organise sports activities during lunch breaks. This allows students to develop healthy habits.

Moreover, exercise can improve students' mental well-being. After studying for several hours, taking part in a physical activity helps them relax and release stress. As a result, they may feel more energetic when they return to their studies.

In conclusion, exercise benefits both physical and mental health. Schools should provide more opportunities for students to stay active.

由普通句子升級

普通寫法拓展後
Exercise is good.Regular exercise is beneficial to students' physical and mental health.
It makes us happy.It helps students relieve stress and improve their mood, especially after a demanding school day.
There are many problems.One major concern is that excessive screen time may lead to sleep problems and a lack of physical activity.

記住：不要為了「高級」而硬塞生字。準確、自然、切題，比堆砌複雜句更重要。

寫完自我檢查

  • 有沒有回應題目每一個 bullet point？
  • 每段有沒有中心句和解釋／例子？
  • 格式、稱呼、語氣是否符合讀者？
  • 時態、主謂一致、單複數、拼字有沒有錯？
  • 有沒有重複使用 very good / bad / important？可否換成準確字眼？
